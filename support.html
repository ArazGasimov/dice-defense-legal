How to play

Pick an element to summon a tower onto the board, tap a tower to upgrade it, and defend against waves of enemies. If an enemy reaches the end, it damages your castle. Survive as long as you can and beat your best score.

Frequently asked

How do I unlock new dice? Open chests to collect shards. Once you have enough shards for a die, it unlocks. You can also craft a die instantly using dust.

Is it pay-to-win? No. Rarer dice are more situational, not stronger. Every unlocked die is balanced to the same power.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device. Reinstalling or clearing app data will reset it.

Report a bug from inside the app: Settings → “Report a bug”. It opens your email app with the details filled in.
