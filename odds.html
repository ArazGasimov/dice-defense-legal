Chest & Drop Odds — Dice Defense

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Last updated: 10 November 2026
  • Every Drop and chest gives random items. This page lists all the odds and rules. The game shows the same odds, calculated for your collection and counters, before you open: tap ODDS (or %) on the Chests screen or in the Shop.
  • Same odds for everyone. Paying never changes any odds, limits or counters.
  • Drops are earned by playing and are never sold for money. A Gold Chest is opened with gold; gold can also be obtained with purchased gems, so a Gold Chest counts as a paid random item.
  • Results have no cash value and cannot be traded or sold. We do not award prizes for collecting a complete set of items from chests.
  • Percentages are exact values used by the game. Small values are shown with up to three decimal places and are never rounded to 0%.

2. Daily win Drops — which tier you get

Each day you have 5 win slots. Each “win” fills one slot and gives one Drop. A win is: winning a Campaign level; reaching wave 8 in Arcade; or winning a Ranked match (a Ranked loss that reaches wave 8 also counts). Slots reset at local midnight; Drops you already received never expire.

The Drop’s tier is decided when the Drop is given and stored. The opening animation only reveals it — tapping does not change the result.

The first slot of each day is always Silver or better. Drops from Campaign stars, the Dice Pass and play-day milestones have the fixed tier shown in section 1 (no random tier).

3. Die rolls

3.1 Rarity of each roll

RarityChance per roll
Common55%
Rare32%
Epic11%
Legendary2%
  • Legendary guarantee: a Legendary roll is guaranteed within every 40 die rolls without one. The counter counts die rolls (not chests), is shared by all Drops and chests, and resets when you get a Legendary roll. The game shows how many rolls are left.
  • The guaranteed Epic roll (Ruby Drop) and Legendary roll (Diamond Drop) are extra rolls of that rarity. A guaranteed Legendary roll also resets the Legendary counter.

3.2 Which die you get

  • After the rarity is decided: if you do not own any die of that rarity yet, the roll goes 100% to your chosen Next die of that rarity (you choose it in the Collection; if you have not chosen, the first locked die of that rarity is used).
  • Otherwise, 20% of the roll goes to your Next die and 80% to one of the dice of that rarity you already own (all equally likely; within one opening the game avoids repeating the same die where possible).
  • If every die of that rarity is already unlocked, the roll is always one of your own dice.
  • Dice that return in a later season are not in any Drop or chest until their season starts (shown in the Collection).

  • New players: for your first 5 Drops or Gold Chests, the emotion chance is doubled; if none of the first 4 gave an emotion, the 5th one gives one for sure. Die odds are not changed.

6. Skin chance (extra, on top) and skin guarantee

The skin chance grows with every opening that gives no skin: chance = starting chance + increase × (openings without a skin), up to the maximum.

TypeStarting chanceIncrease per opening without a skinMaximum
Bronze Drop0.05%+0.001%0.55%

Ruby Drop1%+0.005%4%
Diamond Drop3%+0.01%10%
Gold Chest and Big Chest0.5%+0.004%2.5%
  • Guarantee: if 499 openings in a row gave no skin, the 500th opening gives a skin (100%).
  • The “openings without a skin” counter is shared by all Drops and chests and resets to 0 when you get a skin. The game shows your current counter and your current chance.
  • Which skin: only skins for dice you own and do not have yet (no duplicates). Skin rarity: Standard 45% · Rare 35% · Epic 15% · Legendary 5%, counted only among the rarities you can still get (the others are shared out proportionally); then equally within that rarity.
  • Signature skins are never in Drops or chests. Season skins (skins of a season’s featured die) are available in the Dice Pass during that season and the next one; later they may also appear in the Shop and in Drops and chests.
  • If there are no skins left for you to get, there is no skin roll and the counter waits.

7. Changes to these odds

We will announce any change to the odds or rules on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect, in the game and here. Your counters (Legendary and skin) are kept when odds change. Per-item odds also change automatically when you unlock dice, emotions or skins, or when dice return in a new season; the in-game ODDS screen always shows your current values.
