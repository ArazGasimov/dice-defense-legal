Privacy Policy — Dice Defense

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Dice Defense (“the app”, “we”) is a single-player mobile game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains, in plain language, what data the app does and does not handle.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

All game data — your progress, best score, collection, decks, currencies, and settings — is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the app removes this data.

Bug reports

If you tap “Report a bug”, the app opens your own email application with a pre-filled message that includes basic technical details (the current screen, wave, score, your device/browser identifier). Nothing is sent automatically — you choose whether to send the email, and it is sent from your email account directly to our support address. The app itself does not collect or store this information. Please do not include personal information in the free-text part of the report.

Permissions

The app uses device vibration (haptics) for gameplay feedback. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will be revised.
